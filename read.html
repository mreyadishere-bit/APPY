The Ordinary Weeks

2026 · six entries

3 February

Bought a second-hand lamp for the desk. The switch is stiff and makes a noise like a door closing. I have already started to like it.

9 February

Walked the long way home past the allotments. Someone has planted runner beans in a perfect line. I stood there longer than a person should stand looking at beans.

17 February

Tried to write in the morning. Ended up cleaning the kitchen instead. The page stayed blank but the sink has never been shinier.

24 February

Rain all day. Read the same paragraph four times. Gave up and watched it come down.

2 March

The man at the corner shop remembered I take the same bread. Small thing. Made the whole morning.

11 March

Sat on the step with tea. Nothing happened for twenty minutes. It was the best part of the day.

Moving House

June 2026 · four entries

14 June

Six boxes labelled kitchen. None of them contain anything I need.

16 June

First night. The flat makes noises I don't know yet. Every one of them woke me.

21 June

Found the box with the mugs. Had tea from the right mug for the first time in a week. Felt like arriving.

28 June

The neighbour's cat has decided the windowsill is hers. I have decided not to argue.

Winter, Slowly

December 2026 · four entries

2 December

Dark by four. I have stopped pretending to like it.

9 December

Soup, again. The recipe is the same one my mother used and it still doesn't taste like hers.

18 December

Frost on the inside of the window this morning. Wrote my name in it and immediately felt foolish.

31 December

Didn't stay up. Woke at half eleven to the sound of other people's fireworks, and went back to sleep content.
